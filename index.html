CF-Logics

Detecting institutional logics in community foundation annual reports

Browser tools accompanying the proposal Detecting Concepts in Organizational Language: A Hybrid AI & Human-in-the-Loop Approach for “Making Words Count” (Dressel, Gomez Teijeiro & LePere-Schloop).

Human labeling interface

Code screened report chunks with the same element and logic questions the AI agent follows. Upload a screening CSV from out/screening/; your coding is exported as CSV and never leaves your browser.

Human vs. AI agreement dashboard

Agreement between the expert coder and the AI agent on three pilot reports: chunk-matched Cohen's kappa, flexible chunk-vicinity agreement, and report-level logic profiles, pooled and per report.
Code, data and method: github.com/CF-Logics-Project/CF-Logics
