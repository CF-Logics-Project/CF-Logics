In 2021, The North East Community Foundation saw a record number of new funds, scholarships and charitable gift annuities established including:

## The Clare Wilson Honorary Fund

To honor his legacy of service, Fuller Hose Company established the Clare Wilson Fund. The fund helps the fire company in two critical ways: meeting immediate equipment needs and ensuring that funds will be there in the future to support the changing needs of the community. In December 2021, thanks to the Clare Wilson Fund, the department received a much-needed new piece of equipment - an F550 multipurpose truck that will assist in brush fires and water rescues.

## The John Manville &amp; Frieda Bodendorfer Phillips Fund

The  fund  was  created  by  Deb  Phillips  and  Jack  Russell  to  honor  family members'  contributions  to  the  community.  Funds  will  be  used  to  support qualified and deserving non-profit organizations whose activities and mission address agriculture and/or food related education, projects and needs.

## The Miller Family Scholarship Fund

For decades, the late Bob Miller and his family have provided scholarships to students in North East. In 2021, they opted to continue this long-standing tradition by partnering with The North East Community Foundation. The Miller Family Scholarship is unique in that it is available to students of any age pursuing 2-year, 4-year or vocational education.

## The Susan Valone MD Memorial Scholarship

Susan  was  raised  in  North  East  and  went  on  to  become  an  infectious disease specialist  in  Colorado.  Her  family  established  the  scholarship  in her memory. It will be awarded to students entering a career in healthcare.

## The Jane Novak Zavasky Scholarship Fund

Former North East Middle School English teacher Jane Zavasky and her family established a new scholarship in 2021. It will be awarded to students who are pursuing a degree in education with  preference will be given to those who attended North East Middle School.

## The Fuller Hose Co. #1 Fund

The Fuller Hose Company #1 was established on December 31 of 1885 as a volunteer fire company serving the citizens of North East and beyond. The fund was established to ensure the future public safety needs of the growing North East community can be met. Specifically the fund addresses future apparatus replacement costs, as fire departments are encouraged to replace front line fire engines every 20 years from the date of manufacture. Fuller Hose Company #1 currently operates two engines, one tanker, one utility truck, one brush truck and two boats.

## A letter from the Chairman

## Dear members of the North East community,

When I joined the board of The North East Community Foundation it was 2001, I was 36 and the Foundation felt blessed to have $250,000.

Recently NECF celebrated two significant milestones - our 30th year impacting the quality of life in North East and an endowment that has grown to more than $10 million.

Thinking of all that has been done over the last 30 years and all who have contributed their time, talents and resources to leave a lasting legacy on our community is humbling.

It's been said: with great power comes great responsibility. Which is why in 2021 NECF began its own strategic visioning process. We now have the funds to make meaningful and transformational change. Therefore, we needed a vision, board structure and strategic direction to support it.

Because collaboration is key to the success of any community or initiative, we started the process by listening. Over the course of several months, the task force committee sat down with members of local government, nonprofits and agencies to gauge our community and organization's strengths, weaknesses, needs and opportunities.

After nearly a year of reflection and planning, NECF is happy to release our new strategic direction. You may view the document on our website at www.necommunityfoundation.org , but here are a few highlights:

Our mission is to improve the quality of life in and around North East by identifying, evaluating and addressing community issues, building charitable endowments and by promoting philanthropic and community leadership.

## Additionally our vision includes:

-  To be recognized as a valued stakeholder in the future of North East
-    T o be a trusted custodian of resources to fulfill the philanthropic intent of our donors
-  To create transformational grants in support of our non-profits
-    T o actively promote community leadership, civic awareness and public engagement

Please don't hesitate to reach out to the members of our board with questions you have about our new strategic direction, The North East Community Foundation or how you can join in our efforts.

It is an honor to partner with donors and the community to make North East better for our residents today - and well into the future.

In 2021, with the help of our donors, NECF was able to grant nearly $285,000 including:

## More than $78,000 to education

- Supporting libraries and schools, providing college scholarships and funds for summer camp experiences in the arts and nature

## More than $92,000 to basic health and safety needs

- Firefighters and first responders, nursing and hospice care, food, clothing and other essentials. In 2021, a new need was addressed:  to meet basic food and hygiene needs of students in the North East School District by partnering with the school and Second Harvest Food Bank to offer backpacks to bridge the gap when kids get hungry over the weekend. Another partnership with Project Love Inc. provided basic hygiene supplies.

## More than $70,000 to enrich our community and preserve our unique history and downtown

- Offering arts and culture, and family fun through free outdoor concerts by the Erie Philharmonic in Gibson Park, entertainment for Picnic in the Park and the Saturday Open Market, July 4 fireworks and an expanded annual holiday light up celebration. Funds provided to North East in Bloom brought new planters and flowers to beautify downtown, along with a new mural.
